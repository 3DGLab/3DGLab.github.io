We welcome students who want to do research on 3D shapes and geometric algorithms. The lab is in the [School of Computer Science]({{ site.affiliation.school_url }}) at [Hanyang University ERICA]({{ site.affiliation.university_url }}), and we accept MS and PhD students. Undergraduate students who want research experience can also contact us.

In the lab, you work with Prof. Q Youn Hong on a research problem from the first idea to the paper. You design a geometric method, write the code for it, and test it on 3D models. We publish our results in journals and conferences such as _Computer-Aided Design_, _Computer Aided Geometric Design_, Pacific Graphics, and SIGGRAPH Asia. The [Publications]({{ '/publications/' | relative_url }}) page shows our recent work.

Courses in linear algebra, computer graphics, and programming are good preparation. An interest in mathematics and in 3D shapes is more important than previous research experience.

## Research areas

- 3D graphics
- Shape modeling and understanding
- Shape design for 3D printing
- Computer-aided design
