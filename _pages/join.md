If you want to do research on 3D shapes and geometric algorithms, we would be glad to hear from you. The lab is part of the [School of Computer Science]({{ site.affiliation.school_url }}) at [Hanyang University ERICA]({{ site.affiliation.university_url }}). You can join us as an MS or PhD student, and undergraduates looking for research experience are welcome to get in touch too.

As a member of the lab, you will work with Prof. Q Youn Hong and take a research problem from the first idea all the way to a paper. Along the way, you will design a geometric method, implement it, and test it on 3D models. We publish our results in journals and conferences. To see what we have been working on, take a look at our [Publications]({{ '/publications/' | relative_url }}) page.

Courses in linear algebra, computer graphics, and programming are good preparation. An interest in mathematics and in 3D shapes is more important than previous research experience.

## Research areas

- 3D graphics
- Shape modeling and understanding
- Shape design for 3D printing
- Computer-aided design
