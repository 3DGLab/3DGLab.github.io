The 3D Graphics Lab (3DGLab) is a research group in the [School of Computer Science]({{ site.affiliation.school_url }}) at [Hanyang University ERICA]({{ site.affiliation.university_url }}). [Prof. Q Youn Hong]({{ '/people/professor/' | relative_url }}) leads the lab.

We study how to model, understand, and process 3D shapes. Our methods represent the surface of an object and also its interior structure, with mathematics and algorithms. We connect these representations to applications in computer-aided design, 3D printing, and 3D scene representation.

We welcome new students. If you want to work with us, read the [Join Us]({{ '/join/' | relative_url }}) page.
